## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an executive-grade, field-authentic aesthetic tailored specifically for Brazilian corporate agribusiness leadership. It discards cliché "hacker terminal" dark modes and glowing neon cybernetics in favor of an organic, prestige executive atmosphere: deep forest slate underpinnings, refined crop greens, and sun-cured harvest tones.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Executive Earth & Canopy:** Rich, low-luminance graphite-olive backgrounds paired with tactile agricultural pigments (grain gold, dew blue, sugarcane emerald).
- **Tone:** Decisive, data-dense, highly reliable, and rooted in the operational realities of massive landholdings, logistics chains, and grain markets.
- **Visual Weight:** Balanced density with strong hierarchy, subtle hairline borders, and restrained glow. Surfaces feel like polished dark stone and dense forest floor rather than synthetic matrix grids.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark slate and deep canopy tones to reduce eye fatigue during continuous executive monitoring while retaining natural warmth.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#22c55e` / `#16a34a`):** Harvest/Canopy Green. Used for primary calls-to-action, positive yield trends, target thresholds, and core system accents.
- **Secondary (`#f59e0b` / `#d97706`):** Mature Crop & Soil Amber. Represents soybean harvest readiness, financial commodity alerts, neutral warnings, and intermediate phases.
- **Tertiary (`#0284c7` / `#38bdf8`):** Dew & Rainfall Blue. Reserved for pluviometry indices, irrigation telemetry, water stress indicators, and atmospheric data.
- **Destructive / High Risk (`#ef4444` / `#dc2626`):** Rust Terracotta. Signals severe pest infestation, extreme drought risk, equipment failure, or negative margins.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - `bg-canvas`: `#0d1612` (deepest forest-slate substrate)
  - `bg-surface-subtle`: `#131f1a` (sidebar and primary shell structure)
  - `bg-surface-elevated`: `#1a2923` (executive metric cards, data modules)
  - `bg-surface-highlight`: `#22362f` (hover states, modal overlays, active chips)
  - `border-hairline`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` or `#2a3f37`
  - `text-primary`: `#f0fdf4` (crisp, tinted off-white)
  - `text-secondary`: `#9cb8aa` (muted sage-grey)
  - `text-tertiary`: `#5d7d70` (subtle captions and metadata)

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines, labels, and analytical metrics. The font delivers clean, architectural geometry with modern humanist curves that bridge corporate poise and contemporary tech without resembling a monospace terminal.

### Usage Standards
- **Numerical Hierarchy:** All metric values (`metric-xl`, `headline-md`) use tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jitter in live sensor and commodity market feeds.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Category badges, operational tags, and metric headers use `label-md` with slight uppercase treatment and `letterSpacing: 0.04em` for rapid visual scanning across heavy dashboards.
- **Contrast & Hierarchy:** Primary headings avoid pure white; they sit in soft sage-white (`#f0fdf4`) to harmonize with the deep olive substrate.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an executive workbench layout designed to eliminate navigational ambiguity and focus senior decision-makers on mission-critical insight.

### Structural Shell Architecture
- **Persistent Left Rail Navigation:** The dedicated primary navigation engine of the platform. Fixed at `260px` width (collapsible to `76px` on tablet/dense mode). All high-level modules (Fazendas, Safra & Talhões, Índices Climáticos, Logística & Frete, ESG & Conformidade) reside here. No top-level tab redundancy.
- **Executive Contextual Header:** A fixed 64px top bar strictly dedicated to operational scope: Group / Holding switcher, Unit/Farm selector (`e.g., Fazenda Santa Helena - MT`), global search, sync indicator, and executive profile menu.
- **12-Column Responsive Fluid Grid:** Canvas modules use dynamic auto-fit grids spanning 12 columns on desktop (`>= 1280px`), transitioning to 6 columns on tablet (`>= 768px`) and 1 column on mobile (`< 768px`).

### Vertical Rhythm & Density
- Component inner margins use `space-md` (`1rem`) for operational data rows and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) for high-level summary cards.
- Screen sections preserve a clear separation of `space-xl` (`2rem`) to maintain executive breathing room without wasteful negative space.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through structural tonal stratification rather than harsh drop shadows or neon blurs.

### Surface Stratification
1. **Canvas (`#0d1612`):** Ground plane representing the base level.
2. **Sidebar & Static Rail (`#131f1a`):** Structurally anchored; separated from canvas by a vertical 1px hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
3. **Data Surfaces & Cards (`#1a2923`):** Elevated modules with a crisp 1px border (`#2a3f37`) and an ambient, low-opacity olive shadow (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(5, 12, 8, 0.5)`).
4. **Interactive Hover & Overlay Tiers (`#22362f`):** Used for table row selections, focused elements, and dropdown flyouts.
5. **Modals & Drawers (`#15241e`):** Cast a deep atmospheric shadow (`0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`), paired with a frosted backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `#0d1612` at 80% opacity).

### Glass & Lighting Disciplines
Avoid intense colored neon edge glow. Use gentle hairline borders (`border-t: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) on elevated containers to replicate the natural top-lit effect of premium hardware consoles.

## Shapes

The design system implements balanced geometric corners (`roundedness: 2`). This avoids the toy-like softness of full pills while steering clear of cold, industrial sharp rectangles.

### Radius Token Assignments
- **Core Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Tag Chips):** `0.5rem` (`rounded-md` / 8px).
- **Cards, Panels, and Modular Widgets:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg` / 16px).
- **Dialogs, Flyout Menus, and Drawers:** `1rem` to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl` / 24px).
- **Status Dots & Circular Avatars:** Full circle (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Harvest Green):** Background `#22c55e`, foreground `#071a10` (deep contrast), font weight 600. Hover state `#16a34a` with subtle top highlight. Never glowing or flashing.
- **Secondary (Soil Slate):** Background `#22362f`, border `1px solid #334d43`, foreground `#f0fdf4`. Hover background `#2c463b`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, foreground `#9cb8aa`. Hover foreground `#f0fdf4` with subtle background tint `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.08)`.

### Executive Data Cards (KPI Widgets)
- Built on `#1a2923` with `#2a3f37` hairline borders.
- Top section houses the metric label in `label-md` uppercase (`#9cb8aa`), alongside operational unit tags (e.g., `sc/ha`, `mm/24h`, `R$/sc`).
- Metric value rendered in `metric-xl` (`#f0fdf4`), accompanied by trend badges:
  - Positive: Green background (`rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`), text `#22c55e`.
  - Attention / Neutral: Amber background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`), text `#f59e0b`.
  - Alert: Rust background (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`), text `#ef4444`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#131f1a`, border `1px solid #2a3f37`, text `#f0fdf4`, placeholder `#5d7d70`.
- Height: 40px for desktop forms, 44px for primary filter dropdowns.
- Active/Focus State: Border shifts to `#22c55e` with an ambient 2px green focus ring (`rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.2)`).

### Chips & Semantic Status Badges
- Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.625rem`), font `label-md`.
- **Safra / Produtividade:** `#10b981` text over `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` base.
- **Seca / Maturação:** `#f59e0b` text over `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)` base.
- **Pluviometria / Clima:** `#38bdf8` text over `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)` base.
- **Risco / Praga:** `#ef4444` text over `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)` base.

### Navigation Rail (Sidebar)
- Background `#131f1a` with 1px border on the right (`#1f3029`).
- Navigation links use 40px height, with icons sized at 20px. Default text color `#9cb8aa`.
- Active link: Rich `#1a2923` background with an emerald left indicator strip (3px width, `#22c55e`) and crisp `#f0fdf4` text.

### Executive Table / Data Grid
- Header row: `#131f1a`, border bottom `1px solid #2a3f37`, text `label-md` in `#9cb8aa`.
- Data rows: `#1a2923`, row height 48px, subtle bottom border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).
- Hover state: Background transitions smoothly to `#22362f`.
